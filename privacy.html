Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Data Processing

FreeTool.site is committed to protecting your privacy. All tools on this website process data entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data you enter into our tools. Your text, passwords, colors, URLs, and other data never leave your device.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or Plausible Analytics) to understand aggregate visitor counts and popular pages. These analytics do not use cookies and do not track individual users.

Advertising

We may display advertisements through third-party ad networks such as Google AdSense. These ad networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can manage your ad preferences through your browser settings or by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Cookies

This site stores only your theme preference (light/dark mode) in your browser's localStorage. We do not use tracking cookies. Third-party ad networks may set their own cookies.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at contact@freetool.site.
