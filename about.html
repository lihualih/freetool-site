About FreeTool.site

Free online tools for developers, writers, and everyone.

Our Mission

FreeTool.site provides free, fast, and privacy-friendly online tools. We believe useful developer and productivity tools should be accessible to everyone without signup walls, paywalls, or privacy concerns.

Why Choose Us?

  • 100% Free — No hidden fees, no premium tiers
  • Privacy First — All processing happens in your browser. We never see your data.
  • No Signup — Use any tool instantly without creating an account
  • Fast — Lightweight pages load instantly, even on slow connections
  • Works Everywhere — Responsive design works on desktop, tablet, and mobile

Available Tools

  • JSON Formatter & Validator — Beautify and validate JSON
  • Word & Character Counter — Count words, characters, sentences
  • Password Generator — Generate secure passwords
  • Color Converter — Convert HEX, RGB, HSL colors
  • Base64 Encoder/Decoder — Encode and decode Base64
  • URL Encoder/Decoder — Encode and decode URLs
  • Unix Timestamp Converter — Convert timestamps to dates
  • QR Code Generator — Generate QR codes from text or URLs
